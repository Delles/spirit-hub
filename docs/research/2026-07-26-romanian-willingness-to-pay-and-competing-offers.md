# Romanian willingness-to-pay and competing offers

**Research date and access date for every web source below:** 2026-07-26

**Ticket:** [Map Romanian willingness-to-pay and competing offers](https://github.com/Delles/spirit-hub/issues/25)

**Market:** Romanian-language paid digital outcomes for identity/current-cycle clarity, relationship
clarity, career-fork clarity, and meaning/personal guidance

## Decision summary

Romanian suppliers are actively asking for money across all four outcomes, but public evidence
of what buyers actually pay is uneven. The strongest observable retail signal is not a consultant's
price page. It is a pair of products with platform or verified-purchase evidence:

- Zodiacool lists a personalized, roughly 24-page numerology PDF at **130 lei**, with **71
  verified-purchase ratings** averaging 4.69/5. This is the cleanest product-specific evidence that
  Romanian customers have paid for an asynchronous personalized identity report
  ([source](https://www.zodiacool.ro/magazin-online/numerograma-numerologie-calcul/pd/n-145),
  accessed 2026-07-26).
- The Romanian App Store lists Secretul Numerelor in-app purchases at **50 lei/month, 290 lei/year,
  359.99 lei lifetime**, and personalized/current-cycle/career products at **269.99–390 lei**. The
  listing has 596 ratings at 4.9/5, and two published reviews explicitly mention purchases; one
  calls a then-40-lei monthly upgrade worthwhile and says the reviewer intended to buy lifetime
  access. The review price is historical, while the current platform price is 50 lei/month
  ([source](https://apps.apple.com/ro/app/the-secret-of-numbers/id1607472166?l=ro&platform=ipad),
  accessed 2026-07-26).

Those observations demonstrate purchases, not a market-wide willingness-to-pay distribution.
Most other numbers in this note are **asking prices only**. They show active positioning and
available substitutes, but they do not reveal unit sales, conversion, refunds, or price elasticity.

The most defensible price-position inference is:

- a compact personalized PDF can sell at **130 lei**;
- a content-rich Romanian numerology app can monetize at **50 lei/month** and offer larger
  one-time products around **270–390 lei**;
- human-produced asynchronous reports currently ask roughly **80–510 lei**;
- live 25–90 minute clarity sessions ask roughly **210–660 lei**, with relationship and premium
  astrology offers reaching **800–1,190 lei**;
- recurring current-cycle guidance is visibly offered at **69, 99, and 159 lei/month**.

These are observed positions, not recommended SpiritHub prices. SpiritHub still needs a real
checkout test with Romanian strangers before treating any price as validated for its own offer.

## Evidence standard

This research uses competitors' own Romanian offer, checkout, delivery, policy, and review pages;
Apple and Google marketplace listings are used as the system of record for app prices, ratings,
reviews, and install bands. No secondary articles are used.

Evidence is classified as follows:

1. **Purchase-linked evidence:** a platform review explicitly describes buying, a seller marks
   ratings as verified purchases, or a booking platform shows completed-customer reviews.
2. **Adoption evidence:** installs, ratings, or reviews establish product use, but do not show which
   users paid or how much.
3. **Seller evidence:** a vendor claim, self-published testimonial, “popular” badge, or live price.
   This establishes positioning or supply, not demand.
4. **Inference:** a synthesis across cited observations. It is labeled and must be validated.

## What the evidence says about willingness to pay

### Demonstrated more strongly

1. **A Romanian personalized numerology PDF at 130 lei has purchase-linked evidence.** Zodiacool's
   71 verified-purchase ratings are not unit sales, but they are stronger than self-published
   testimonials and make 130 lei the best public asynchronous-report anchor in this scan
   ([source](https://www.zodiacool.ro/magazin-online/numerograma-numerologie-calcul/pd/n-145),
   accessed 2026-07-26).
2. **A Romanian numerology app supports both recurring and high one-time prices.** Apple currently
   exposes 50 lei monthly, 290 lei yearly, 359.99 lei lifetime and 269.99–390 lei specialized
   products. Published reviews explicitly mention paying, but Apple does not expose how many
   ratings came from payers or which SKU they bought
   ([source](https://apps.apple.com/ro/app/the-secret-of-numbers/id1607472166?l=ro&platform=ipad),
   accessed 2026-07-26).
3. **Broad product use is substantial.** Google Play shows 100K+ installs and thousands of reviews
   for the same Romanian-developed app. This supports demand for the combined identity/cycle/
   compatibility utility, not a specific paid conversion rate
   ([source](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=ro&id=ro.secretulnumerelor.app),
   accessed 2026-07-26).

### Demonstrated weakly or not at all

- Seller pages at 180–1,190 lei establish active offers, not sales.
- A review count of one, four or six is compatible with purchases but too small to establish a
  stable price band.
- Self-published testimonials and vendor-client totals may include free work, other products,
  courses or sessions.
- “Best seller,” “recommended,” “popular,” crossed-out prices and bundles are merchandising unless
  backed by transaction data.
- No public source in this scan exposes Romanian unit sales, checkout conversion, refunds,
  retention, or a price experiment for an exact SpiritHub-like product.

## Price architecture and format patterns

The observed market is organized less by topic than by **human involvement and retained access**:

| Format | Observed live positions | What appears to justify the price |
|---|---|---|
| App membership | 50 lei monthly; 190/6 months; 290/year; 359.99 lifetime | Breadth, repeated use, daily/current-cycle utility, saved access |
| Compact or manually produced PDF | 80, 111, 130, 180 lei | Personalization, portable/revisitable artifact, specialist framing |
| Recorded interpretation | 147 for a reusable content library; 350–510 for personalized audio/video | Voice and perceived expert synthesis without a scheduled live call |
| Short live clarity | 210–310 lei for 25–50 minutes | Immediacy, personalization, direct questions |
| Live + artifact | 333–660 lei for 60–90 minutes, often with PDF/recording | Human dialogue, richer synthesis, permanent artifact |
| Premium relationship/family or specialist report | 600–1,190 lei | Multiple profiles, named practitioner, high-touch support |

This table is an **inference from cited offers**, not a demand curve. The best-supported point is the
130-lei PDF; the app provides credible recurring and one-time anchors but not SKU-level sales.

## Observable differentiation gaps for SpiritHub

### 1. Instant, coherent, revisitable output

Many lower/mid-price reports take 3–21 days, while live products require scheduling. The leading
app is instant but broad and tiered. SpiritHub can differentiate with an immediate Romanian web/PDF
artifact that connects stable identity themes, the current cycle, reflection prompts and one
user-chosen next step. “Instant” should include an honest generation-time promise and no invented
human review.

### 2. Reflection without claims about another person's mind

Relationship tarot incumbents explicitly sell partner intentions, fidelity, future timing and
destiny. A SpiritHub relationship offer can own a narrower, defensible outcome: separate facts from
interpretations, identify the user's needs and boundaries, and prepare one respectful conversation.
It must not imply access to a third party's thoughts or future.

### 3. A one-time current-cycle product between free utility and subscription

The market offers both monthly subscriptions (50–159 lei/month) and manual annual/current-cycle
readings (180–333+ lei). There is visible room to test a one-time, instant current-cycle report with
permanent access, without making the user start a subscription. This is a gap hypothesis, not proof
that buyers prefer one-time payment.

### 4. Decision support rather than a label

Career competitors either return an assessment profile or add a human interpretation session.
SpiritHub can test a non-psychometric workbook that compares live options, values, constraints,
evidence and reversible seven-day experiments. It should never call itself a validated vocational
or personality test unless it actually licenses and validates one.

### 5. Safety and data-use clarity as product value

The better competitor pages disclose GDPR, payment handling, timing and limits; Alchimia Lunii also
sets therapy/crisis and adult-consent boundaries
([source](https://alchimialunii.ro/), accessed 2026-07-26). SpiritHub can make these visible before
payment: symbolic/not deterministic; not medical, legal, financial or psychological advice; no
guaranteed prediction; exact data retention/deletion; exact delivery; refund rule; and a refusal to
analyze non-consenting adults.

### 6. Show the artifact before asking for payment

Free previews exist at CitireDestin and Human Design Romania, while several expensive pages sell
mainly through practitioner authority and testimonials
([CitireDestin](https://citiredestin.ro/preturi),
[Human Design Romania](https://humandesignro.ro/produs/raport-intro-human-design/), both accessed
2026-07-26). SpiritHub can expose a realistic sample section, data inputs, page/section outline and
safety limits before checkout. The sample must represent the paid depth rather than act as vague
teaser copy.

## Important stale-price warning

CitireDestin still displays **47.40 RON** for a 20+ page Numerologie Premium PDF and **89.40 RON**
for a 30+ page natal-chart PDF. The same page explicitly says those are -40% launch prices valid
only through **30 May 2026**, almost two months before this research date. It also says premium
readings remain permanently in an account and are PDF-downloadable, while only the free basic
numerology/astrology preview is no-account. Therefore:

- 47.40 and 89.40 RON are **stale displayed promotional prices**, not confidently current active
  prices;
- the mathematically implied pre-discount prices would be 79 and 149 RON, but that is an inference,
  not a price currently labeled as payable;
- neither number should be used as the leading willingness-to-pay anchor until a checkout confirms
  the charged amount.

Source: [CitireDestin pricing page](https://citiredestin.ro/preturi), accessed 2026-07-26.

## Validation implications for SpiritHub

The public market record is good enough to justify a price test, not a build commitment.

For an instant identity + current-cycle artifact, a sensible research design would expose separate
Romanian cohorts to **59 / 89 / 129 lei**, keeping the product and acquisition source constant.
That ladder spans below the verified 130-lei manual PDF while testing whether immediacy, coherent
synthesis and a safe/revisitable artifact can compensate for the absence of a named human analyst.
This is a **SpiritHub experiment proposal**, not a market finding.

Record separately:

- unique visitors who saw the price;
- sample opens and completed previews;
- checkout starts;
- payment attempts and successful payments;
- fulfillment completions;
- refunds;
- 7- and 30-day revisits;
- whether the buyer reports taking the promised reflective action.

Do not count waitlist clicks, disabled checkout clicks, app installs, or free-result completions as
willingness to pay. A decision to build should depend on completed payments from Romanian strangers
and successful fulfillment without unsafe promises.

## Limitations

- Exact prices are page observations on 2026-07-26 and can change; VAT inclusion is reported only
  where the source makes it clear.
- Apple IAP prices are current platform observations. Ratings are adoption evidence; only reviews
  that explicitly mention buying are treated as purchase-linked.
- Google Play install/review totals are global, not Romania-only.
- Verified ratings establish at least rated purchases, not total units or current-period sales.
- Seller-hosted reviews can be selected, moderated or subject to survivorship bias even when the
  site labels them verified.
- No source exposes Romanian conversion, acquisition cost, refund rate, retention, or buyer income.
- Career assessment sites may require licensed instruments or professional interpretation.
  SpiritHub should not imitate protected tests or imply clinical/psychometric validation.
- The scan is designed around the demand pockets selected in
  [Find the strongest Romanian demand pockets for SpiritHub v2](https://github.com/Delles/spirit-hub/issues/23);
  it is not an exhaustive census of every Romanian astrologer, numerologist, coach, therapist, app
  or course.

## Ticket conclusion

Romanian-language buyers demonstrably pay for personalized numerology in at least two digital
contexts: a 130-lei asynchronous PDF with 71 verified-purchase ratings, and a numerology app with
current 50-lei monthly and 269.99–390-lei one-time products plus purchase-mentioning reviews.
Relationship and career outcomes support much higher live-service asking prices, but public demand
proof is thinner and their value depends heavily on human interpretation.

The clearest SpiritHub opening is not the cheapest generic report. It is an instant, Romanian-native,
transparent and revisitable identity + current-cycle artifact that makes symbolic interpretation
useful without deterministic or third-party claims. Test it against real payment at multiple
prices before building automation, subscriptions or accounts.
